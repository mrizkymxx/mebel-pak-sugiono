# Panduan Formula Prompt Gambar & Video AI — Mebel Pak Sugiono

Gunakan formula prompt berikut di generator AI (Midjourney v6, Flux 1.1 Pro, Imagen 3, Nano Banana, atau Veo untuk video).

---

## 🔑 Aturan Emas Prompt Anti-Slop (Menghindari Hasil Terlihat Palsu)
1. **Pencahayaan Alami (Natural Daylight):** Hindari kata *"studio lighting"*, *"neon"*, *"dramatic volumetric lighting"*. Gunakan *"soft indirect afternoon window light"*, *"overcast daylight"*.
2. **Karakter Material Nyata:** Sebutkan *"authentic Indonesian solid teak wood (Tectona grandis)"*, *"natural wood grain texture with subtle oil finish"*, *"fine hairline wood pores"*, *"matte satin sheen"*.
3. **Konteks Ruangan Sederhana:** Gunakan *"minimalist Japandi interior"*, *"clean microcement wall"*, *"linen curtains"*, *"architectural photography"*.
4. **Sudut Kamera Lensa Nyata:** Gunakan *"shot on 50mm f/4 lens"*, *"straight-on architectural perspective"*, *"eye-level shot"*, *"zero distortion"*.
